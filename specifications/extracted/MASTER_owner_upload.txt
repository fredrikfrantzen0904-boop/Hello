                                            Master specification

 Authority and scope
 You are a specialist implementation engineer for Gold Intelligence Engine (GIE). The owner approves
 material changes; ChatGPT provides architecture and independent review recommendations. Read the
 approved master specification, CLAUDE.md, contracts and decision log before editing. Raise conflicts or
 missing dependencies as change requests; never silently simplify, invent data, or mark placeholders
 complete. Use a dedicated Git branch/worktree; do not overwrite other sessions, merge without owner
 approval, or modify existing Gold Bot, AI trader, breakout bot or GCE baselines. Paper/replay only; build
 execution interfaces but keep real order routing disabled. Do not expose credentials. Record exact commit,
 files, tests, commands, results, limitations and requirement IDs. Submit a PDF review plus
 machine-readable evidence; stop at the review gate.

 G01 | Objective
 XAUUSD exclusively; hybrid scalping and intraday, continuous monitoring during actual broker sessions,
 cautious Asian-session selection, overnight positions when permitted and monitored, no weekend
 exposure. Target at least one valid trade per eligible day and at least 5% average daily net ROI; report
 misses, never force trades or leverage. These are research goals, not established performance.

 G02 | Intelligence
 Six existing engines provide directional scores and evidence: Cavemen, Silver Bullet, Liquidity Hunter, ORB
 + VWAP, VWAP Trend Rider, Sweep & Reversal. Deduplicate correlated events. Build setup-specific
 expected net R, uncertainty, price destinations and flexible trade plans; actual market behavior overrides
 historical expectations. Preserve GCE-B and exact Round 1 baselines.

 G03 | Timing
 Anticipate, prepare, react, protect. Reflex every tick; tactical every tick or second; strategic on completed
 candles and asynchronous background updates. No network LLM or training in critical path. Proposed local
 targets: tick processing <1 ms, protection decision <5 ms, tactical <20 ms; measure p50/p95/p99/max and
 end-to-end delays, reject stale signals. No guarantee of zero latency.

 G04 | Risk
 No arbitrary position count; aggregate gross/net and correlated risk, pending orders, margin, costs and gap
 stress. Adaptive sizing only from validated quality; no martingale, averaging down or widening protective
 stops. Hard protective stops; fast monotonic profit ratchet based on executable net P&L. Compare against
 fixed exits.

 G05 | Daily states
 Normal above -2% until +5%; defensive at -2%, reduced risk and stricter quality; proposed recovery at
 -1.75% and emergency halt at -3%, configurable and subject to validation. Above +5% protect a
 nondecreasing daily profit floor, exceptional setups only with surplus risk budget after costs and open
 exposure. At exactly the floor no new risk. Slippage can breach floors. Track Prague day and actual account
 rules, including other positions.

 G06 | Volatility and external data
 Scheduled news and unexpected volatility mode: prepare scenarios, assess spread, acceleration and
 persistence, reject stale entries. Verify account news restrictions. Observe XAGUSD, DXY, GC futures,
 Treasury yields, real yields, S&P 500, VIX, USDJPY and timestamped news where licensed and available;
 trade only gold. Learn conditional lead/lag, never assume fixed correlations.

 G07 | Learning
 Historical replay and market memory, setup-specific return models, new pattern discovery, rejected and
 missed opportunities, execution and exit learning. No manual-trade learning. Separate decision quality from
 outcomes. Background research isolated from active model; shadow validation, versioned promotion with
 owner approval and rollback. No untested online changes.

 G08 | Reliability
 Build autonomous order lifecycle foundation now, with simulated adapter and live lock. Persistent state,
 restart reconciliation, disconnect/stale-feed fail closed, idempotent orders, broker-side protection design,
 overnight monitoring. Never enable real execution during this project phase.

 G09 | Evidence
 Round 2: 23,481 setups; GCE-B 134 trades +0.462R, 18% day coverage, 4.9% drawdown at 0.5% risk; no
 corrected statistical significance. Learned score 96% coverage but negative recent period. Existing history
 is research only; raw ticks not stored in Round 2. Reconcile 429 vs 473 Cavemen trades, daily breach
 denominators and live delay. Use actual tick data for fast execution claims.

 G10 | Validation
 Chronological purged walk-forward, embargo, training-only transforms, block bootstrap, multiple-testing
 correction, cost/latency stress, untouched forward paper. Report all eligible days, net R/day, mean/median
 ROI, >=5% days, coverage, drawdown, breaches, phase-specific challenge outcomes, failures and
 uncertainty. No cherry-picking.

 Implementation directive
 Start with session 00 only. Owner approves contracts before parallel work. Wave 2: 01, 02, 07, 08. Wave 3:
 03, 04, 05, 06 as dependencies become ready. Wave 4: 09, 10, 11, 12; research and QA start scaffolding
 earlier. Coordinator alone integrates reviewed branches. No live activation. Proposed numerical settings are
 research parameters, not approved production limits.

 Review gate
 Every session delivers requirement IDs, code paths, test commands and outputs, commit hash, data
 provenance, measured performance, blockers and change requests. Status: approved, conditional, rejected
 or unverified. Owner authorizes merge and model promotion.
